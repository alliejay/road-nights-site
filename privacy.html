Road Nights

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Short version: your records stay on your phone. We have no servers and no accounts, and we don’t collect, see or sell your days, expenses, notes or receipt photos. The only outside party is the one that checks your subscription.

What the app stores, and where

Road Nights stores the days you mark, the expenses you enter, your notes, receipt photos you attach and your settings. All of it lives only on your device, inside the app. It isn’t uploaded to us or anyone else. Your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud or computer) may include it, and those are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Backups and exports you create

When you export a PDF or CSV report, or save a backup file, the app hands the file to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, such as Files, iCloud Drive, email or AirDrop. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We don’t receive your name, email or payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a random identifier for your install, your purchase history for this app, and basic technical data such as device model, OS version and IP address. It doesn’t receive any of your records. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Camera, photos and notifications

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to attach a receipt. The app saves a resized copy inside the app.
  • Notifications: the daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no advertising.
  • No location tracking.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

Road Nights is a business record-keeping tool. It isn’t directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.
