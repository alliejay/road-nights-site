Common questions

How is my per diem calculated?

For each day you mark as on the road, Road Nights uses the IRS special meals-and-incidentals rate for the transportation industry in effect on that date: $80 a day in the continental US and $86 outside it for Oct 1, 2026 – Sep 30, 2027 (IRS Notice 2026-60). The days you leave and get back count at 75% by default. The deductible amount is 80% of the total, the limit for drivers under DOT hours-of-service rules. You can change these settings to match your tax preparer.

Why didn’t a day count?

Per diem requires being away overnight. A single day marked on the road with no day next to it looks like a day trip, so it’s flagged and not counted. Mark both the day you left and the day you got back.

Which days do I mark?

Every calendar day you were away from your tax home, including the day you left and the day you returned. Use “Select several days” to mark a whole trip at once.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

If iCloud is on for your iPhone, Road Nights backs up automatically to your own iCloud: there’s nothing to do. On a new or replacement iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID, install Road Nights and it will offer to restore your records, receipts included. Check Settings → Backup in the app: it says when the last iCloud backup happened.

No iCloud? Use Settings → Backup → Save a backup file now and then, and keep it in Files or email it to yourself. Restore it with Settings → Backup → Restore… A restore replaces everything in the app with the backup.

I bought Pro but the app doesn’t show it

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Road Nights. Your records stay in the app either way.

Is this tax advice?

No. Road Nights is a record-keeping aid. Check your situation with a tax professional.
